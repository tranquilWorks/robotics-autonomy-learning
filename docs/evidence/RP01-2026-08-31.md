- Generator replacement is atomic per file, not transactional across the complete fixture bundle; interruption can leave a mixed local tree, which must not be retained unless regeneration and the deterministic fixture comparison pass.

- No MATLAB runtime was available or invoked; numerical fidelity, simulation behavior, controls, estimation, telemetry transport, and protocol replay were not validated.
